# ServiceNow MCP Apps v1.2.0 LinkedIn announcement

Status: Draft - not approved for publication

## Post

ServiceNow MCP Apps v1.2.0 is now available, adding a Knowledge-first support journey inside Microsoft 365 Copilot.

Users can search caller-visible ServiceNow articles, open a ranked result in an interactive MCP Apps widget, and keep trying without losing the thread. After three unresolved attempts, the experience offers incident creation only with explicit consent.

Article results preserve useful source structure for readability and provide a clear handoff to the complete caller-authorized ServiceNow article when images or attachments are omitted. Feedback is written to native, caller-attributed ServiceNow records, while consented incidents can link attempted articles without making incident success depend on those best-effort links.

What is included:

- 27 narrowly scoped MCP tools and 9 interactive widgets across Knowledge, catalog, requests, approvals, and incidents
- Ranked Knowledge search, readable article views, optional feedback comments, and consent-based escalation
- Caller-visible Knowledge access and native, caller-attributed feedback records

I would welcome feedback from teams exploring secure, user-attributed ServiceNow workflows in Copilot.
